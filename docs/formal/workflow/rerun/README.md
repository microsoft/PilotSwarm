# Workflow re-run from a chosen visit (I7 v1)

`WorkflowRerun.tla` models Workflow Re-run from a Chosen Visit (`I7` v1) ahead of store and runtime implementation. A TLC pass is a bounded check, not a proof.

## Relation to `WorkflowRun.tla`

`WorkflowRerun.tla` **restates** (rather than `INSTANCE`s) the unchanged `1.3.0` controller loop (`AdmitExec`/`AdmitRecord`, `CollectExec`/`CollectRecord`, `FinishExec`/`FinishFailExec`, `PollCommand`, `CancelTx`) of `../WorkflowRun.tla` so it can begin from an atomically copied prefix of completed visits (`reusedSet`) and couple that loop with the two-step Start claim/admission transaction, lock-ordered deleter races, compatibility and cut-guard checks (both `run_again` and `continue`, same-IR input/carry byte check, and copied-coordinate divergence), plan budget subtraction, approval consent gating, exact carried inbox filtering over a generated source inbox, receive `wait.totalSeconds` reset, and two-generation `COALESCE` origin flattening.

## Carried inbox: generated source inbox and exact carry (Task 6a)

The model no longer uses a fixed set of hand-picked messages. For `SourceKind = "inbox"`, `Init` chooses #2's inbox `srcInbox` from every combination of shapes of two ordinary messages (`m1`, `m2`) and of the reused spawn's `WorkflowEnded@1` notice (`m_child`): 12 shapes each, 1,728 inboxes. A shape is `[accept, expire, reader]` with `expire > accept`, on abstract instants `1` (before the cut), `2` (`CutTime`, exactly at the cut), `3` (after the cut) and `4` (later):

- `accept` (`accepted_at`) is before, at, or after the cut;
- `expire` (`expires_at`) is exactly at the cut or after it;
- `reader` (`consumed_by`) is `NoNode` (unconsumed), `KeptReader` (#2's receive `R1`, which the re-run reuses) or `DroppedReader` (#2's receive `R2` at the cut, which the re-run runs again).

`SourceKind = "spawn"` keeps one fixed waiting message, and every other kind has an empty inbox, so only the inbox cases grow.

`EligibleCarry(m)` is the design rule (Design §5.4): the messages a fresh run started at the cut would still see, read from the history the cut is taken in (a message's first acceptance and its expiry). An ordinary message is eligible when `accept <= CutTime < expire`. A `WorkflowEnded@1` notice of a reused spawn child is eligible whatever its times; a notice of a child whose spawn #3 does not reuse is never eligible (review I-INBOX). In every case the message must not be consumed by a #2 visit that #3 keeps: `KeptSourceVisits = ExpectedReused \cup ExpectedSupplied` (reused visits including lanes, and the supplied visit `V'`). `TargetInbox` is the code's filter, written separately over #2's stored columns (`StoredAcceptedAt`, `StoredOriginAt`, `StoredExpiresAt`, `CodeCutAt`) so that faults can change it. `Inv_InboxCarryExact` requires `carriedInbox = EligibleCarrySet` once admitted. `Inv_CarriedWereWaiting` (`carriedInbox \subseteq EligibleCarrySet`) alone cannot catch a filter that carries too little. Unconsumed carried messages still expire by restarted TTL after #3 finishes (`ExpireCarriedInbox`, unchanged).

### Fix wave R3 (I-INBOX, RR-1, MSG-1)

- **Stray child notice (I-INBOX, `StrayNotice`).** `m2` is replaced by `m_stray`, a `WorkflowEnded@1` notice of a child whose spawn #3 does not reuse, generated over the same 12 shapes (1,728 inboxes). The fault `CarryNoticeByTime` removes only the time arm's contract discriminator.
- **Second generation, cut inside #2's copied prefix (RR-1, `Generations = 2`, `CutVisitCopied`).** #3's cut is a visit #2 copied from #1, so it keeps its #1 entry time (`CutTime`). Every row of #2's inbox was carried from #1 at #2's admission (`CopyTime = 5`, after all of #1): stored `accepted_at = CopyTime`, a restarted expiry, and `origin_accepted_at = accept`. Such a message was unexpired at #2's own cut and no copied visit read it, so its shapes are `expire = 4` and `reader` `NoNode` or `DroppedReader` (216 inboxes). The ground truth stays `EligibleCarry` (first acceptance in #1's history). `Inv_NoWaitingLostAcrossGenerations` is the no-loss half of `Inv_InboxCarryExact` for this cut: #3 loses no message, among rows carried into the immediate source (#2), that waited at that cut in #1's history. A row of #1 that #2 did not carry is outside it; a re-run reads its immediate source only, never an earlier ancestor. The fault `CarryComparesCopyTime` (the code before `origin_accepted_at`) compares the stored `accepted_at`; it changes nothing in generation 1, where a row has no origin time.
- **The source run's end racing an acceptance (MSG-1, `SourceEndRace`, `EndClock`).** Before the re-run is claimed, #2 is active: its end transaction `E` begins (`EndBegin`, `now()` fixed at `endTxStart`), an acceptance `A` of `m_late` takes #2's session lock and reads the clock after its locks (`AccLock`, `AccCommit`), the clock advances (`SrcClockTick`, at most 3), and `E` commits once the lock is free (`EndCommit`). `EndClock = "begin"` (the code before the fix) stamps `finished_at = endTxStart`; `"locked"` stamps the clock read after the locks. #3 cuts at #2's end; `m_late` waited there exactly when #2 accepted it (event order). `Inv_AcceptedBeforeEnd` requires `accAt <= srcEndAt`. The model abstracts the cut selection: the visit structure of `SourceKind = "inbox"` is reused for #3's admission and take, and only the inbox cut is #2's end, as for `continue`/`supply` from the last top-level visit.

Code-level evidence for the exact microsecond boundaries: `workflow-rerun-start.test.js` ("cuts the carried inbox at the exact microsecond (D-I1)…") seeds a cut at `…000900` and checks accepted at `…000900` (carried) and `…000901` (not carried), and expiring at `…000900` (not carried) and `…000901` (carried), through both `copyRerunInboxIn` and `summarizeRerunCarriedInbox`. Fix wave R3 evidence: `workflow-rerun-store.test.js` ("…never through the ordinary time window (I-INBOX)") excludes a time-eligible stray notice and carries reused notices after the cut and expired at it, through copy and summary; `workflow-rerun-start.test.js` ("…(RR-1: #1 -> #2 -> #3)") re-runs a re-run from its copied `step2` and from its copied `step1` with `continue`, carries the message that waited there with its TTL restarted again, and ("…(MSG-1)") races `finishRun` against `acceptInput` on two connections and checks `accepted_at <= finished_at` and the run-end carry.
